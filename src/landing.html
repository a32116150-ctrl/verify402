How a call works

Three steps, no signup.

01

Ask without paying

Call any paid route with no payment. It answers 402 Payment Required with exact instructions: coin, network, amount, recipient, and the per-call cap.

02

Pay and retry once

Your agent pays in USDC over x402 — Base or Solana — and retries the same request with proof. The payment is the authentication.

03

Get the verdict + receipt

The answer arrives with an evidence bundle: request hash, response hash, pinned block. A lost reply replays free within ten minutes.

Building an agent? The docs have the exact requests. Prefer plain words? The receipts page shows what has and hasn't been proven.
