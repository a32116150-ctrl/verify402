How to order

STEPYOU CALLTHE CATALOG RETURNS
1. curl https://verify402.onrender.com/findings 200 OK. Live verdicts on the majors. No key, no payment, no account.
2. curl -X POST …/verify -H "x-idempotency-key: my-op-1" -d '{"pair":"USDC/WETH"}' HTTP 402 Payment Required — the door, not a wall. The 402 says what coin it accepts, exactly who pays, and caps the call.
3. same call, payment header filled → settle 200 OK, settled. The verdict arrives inside the payment receipt, with the request hash, the response hash and the pinned block. Lose the reply? Retry free for ten minutes, three times. Past that the retry comes back flagged, never silently recharged.
