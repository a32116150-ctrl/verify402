No signup, no key. The payment is the authentication. Send x-idempotency-key on the first call of an operation: a lost reply after settlement replays the same verdict free inside ten minutes, three times. Past the window the retry re-measures with the paywall re-armed and comes back flagged, so the agent can branch, never guess. The spend caps live wallet-side, and the page says so rather than claiming a boundary it cannot enforce.
