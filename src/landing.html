"no_edge" is the product

Most measurement sellers get paid when the answer is yes. This service is built to be paid when the honest answer is no — a pinned-block verdict that says the edge is gone once flash-loan premiums and gas are netted out. In live measurement, mainstream pairs mostly resolve to no_edge. A negative result that survives an independent reproduction is worth more to an agent's budget than any bullish dashboard.

Every paid response ships an evidence_bundle: a hash of the canonical request, a hash of the response, the pinned block and the mode — so any claim made with this tool can be re-derived from the recipe in the repo README, byte for byte.

Recovery, caps, receipts — the honest contract

ConcernWhat actually ships
Payment settled, response lost?Send x-idempotency-key on the first call: the same verdict replays free within 10 minutes (3 replays max). Verdicts are double-keyed — also recorded under a hash of the request content — so a retry with a fresh key is still covered.
Replay window expired?The retry re-measures with the paywall re-armed, and the response carries recovery.remeasured plus the previous response hash — a typed signal, never a silent double charge.
Runaway spending?The companion MCP bridge enforces a hard per-call ceiling and an optional per-day ledger persisted locally; it counts attempted charges, not confirmed settlements, so it errs on the safe side.
Delegated wallets?Stated plainly: the caps live in the signer process. They do not travel with wallets delegated to other agents — mitigation belongs to the delegating owner.
