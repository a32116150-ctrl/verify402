The rules

What a donation does not buy.

  • No priority, no faster answers, no private routes.
  • No influence on a verdict — measurements stay as blunt as today.
  • No promised return, no token, no equity, no financial product.
  • No obligation to support a bad call: send only if the work helped you.

Every donation is acknowledged on the proof page the same way settlement receipts are: a public thank-you always, a transaction link only if the donor wants it named. Current ledger: no donations yet.
