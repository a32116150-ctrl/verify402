Receipts · evidence · verification

An agent payment receipt should be checkable, not just printable.

verify402 returns two kinds of proof. The free gate issues an HMAC-signed receipt bound to the exact intent and request. Paid checks return an evidence bundle with request and response hashes. Both are records you can recompute — neither is a promise that the trade was safe or profitable.

Verify a receipt in Node See public receipts

01 · The free receipt

Bound to the intent, the request and the block.

Every POST /preflight verdict carries a receipt with an intentHash, a requestHash, a pinned block and an HMAC signature. It expires after 90 seconds by default; 15–300 seconds are configurable. If the chain cannot be read, there is no receipt at all.

The receipt proves the gate answered that normalized intent and that request body at that block. It does not prove the bytes were executed, that a seller was honest, or that a token is safe.

02 · Verification boundary

Recompute hashes locally. Validate the signature at the issuer.

You can recompute intentHash and requestHash locally from the exact intent and request. Signature validation needs the issuer's POST /preflight/verify or the shared PREFLIGHT_SECRET. That endpoint checks expiry, re-hashes what you sent, and returns bad_signature for a tampered signature or request_mismatch for an altered request. Require both intentBound and requestBound before your signer moves.

Executor rule

Refuse on BLOCK. Refuse on a missing, expired or unverifiable receipt. Treat CAUTION as an explicit decision, not a pass. The reference guard implements that order and holds no key.

03 · Paid evidence

The same idea, one layer deeper.

Paid responses carry an evidence bundle: the hash of the canonical request, the hash of the response body excluding envelope fields such as cache and recovery, the pinned block and the verdict. A third party can recompute the measurement inputs without trusting the service's prose. The /assure bundle adds a recomputable sha256 over the checks actually supplied at that block.

A receipt is a falsifiable record. If the bytes changed, the hash changes. That is the whole point.

04 · What it does not mean

Not settlement, not reputation, not profit.

A verified preflight receipt does not mean the x402 facilitator settled the payment, that the seller delivered the resource, that the payee wallet is controlled by the seller, or that the quoted trade would make money. Those facts are either outside the gate's reach or listed in notChecked.

Receipts are short-lived by design. Re-call the gate for a fresh decision instead of caching a PASS. For the broader policy, read AI agent payment security.

Next

Store the request with the receipt.

A receipt without the request it was bound to is hard to audit. Keep them together, then verify before you act.
