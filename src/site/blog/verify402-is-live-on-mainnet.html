verify402 is live on mainnet — here is the receipt.

The gate now serves Base mainnet and Solana mainnet at vex402.online, settling real USDC through the Coinbase CDP facilitator. Two end-to-end settlements have moved on-chain as of today. Both were the operator's own money. No external buyer has paid anything yet — and that is the part of this note that matters most.

Update — Glama: green

Same day (Nov 18): Glama has evaluated the server. Dockerfile build is green (23.6s), the listing is claimed, glama.json is valid on master, Maintenance score B, license Apache-2.0, Discovery score 67%, and Glama TDQS 4.4/5 across 3 tools (preflight_gate 4.8, directory_402 4.5, inspect_402 3.9). npm 1.1.5 published; MCP Registry lists 1.1.5; GitHub release mcp-v1.1.5 shipped.

This is the account of a mainnet cutover and not a milestone manufactured to look like one. Everything below is chain-verifiable; everything we did not do is also stated plainly.

What changed, and what did not

  • The service was deployed to a dedicated always-on Linux host (not a free tier), running node 22 with a durable SQLite recovery store — an idempotency record survives a restart. Reboot-tested.
  • It is served at https://vex402.online through an outbound tunnel; no inbound ports are open on the host beyond SSH, and SSH is key-only.
  • The 402 challenge advertises eip155:8453 (Base mainnet) alongside Solana mainnet, paid in USDC, verified and settled by the Coinbase CDP facilitator.
  • /info reports mode: mainnet; /health reports recoveryStore: ok. Chain-verifiable here: https://vex402.online/health.
  • Prices are unchanged. /preflight, /inspect, and /directory remain free. The public demo at https://verify402.onrender.com remains testnet — this page makes no claim about it beyond "it is a demo."

The receipts, precisely

Two settlements have happened through the live path, both by the operator paying the service's own payee (the operator's Phantom base wallet). Both are $0.03 USDC. Both settled under CDP.

What this does not prove

  • Not demand. Zero external buyer has paid anything. The ledger nobody has paid remains the ledger that matters.
  • Not the Bazaar placement. We have not relisted vex402.online in the x402 catalog yet; the stale testnet listings at the old host are not evidence of mainnet traffic.
  • Not that the analysis is correct. The verdicts are measurements, not guarantees; /preflight exists precisely because "the model sounded sure" is not a check.
  • Not your money. The only settled USDC is the operator's. If you pay, you pay for what the challenge asks — check eip155:8453 and the payee before you sign.

How to try it, honestly

For humans and operators of agents: POST https://vex402.online/preflight is free — use it on anything you were about to sign. For builders who want a $0.03 receipt on public record: set VERIFY402_URL=https://vex402.online and VERIFY402_NETWORKS=eip155:8453 and call any paid route from a dedicated hot wallet. The bridge pays the challenge only; it never signs or sends a trade.
