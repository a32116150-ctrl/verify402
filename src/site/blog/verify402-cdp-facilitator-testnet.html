Settlement · 6 min read · Nov 16, 2026

We settle through the Coinbase CDP facilitator — on testnet.

The Bazaar only indexes services that pay through Coinbase's facilitator. verify402 now does — on Base Sepolia, not mainnet; one recorded testnet payment, chain-verified, that you can independently check in the block explorer.

This is the account of a fact drop and not a milestone manufactured to look like one. The change that matters is small and verifiable on the chain, but it's the only door x402 agents actually walk through: the Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) x402 facilitator, which verifies x402 payments and indexes only services that settle through it.

What happened, precisely

  • The service now runs with a Live (Production) CDP key scoped to View + Transfer, with any-IP facilitator access, on Base Sepolia.
  • /info.payment reports facilitator: api.cdp.coinbase.com and discovery: cdp-bazaar-eligible.
  • One testnet USDC payment settled end-to-end through the facilitator — 0.03 USDC, buyer → 0xAeEF… (payTo), tx 0x6a31aab9ab567ec9f28aa77114c3e8128fae7689986a3eed1bf8e5e996c099f2, block 59,630,136.

Live on Base Sepolia — not live on mainnet, not paid by strangers, and not listed in the Bazaar yet. The catalog does not index us at this moment; we can show you the search returning resources: [] for verify402 right now, and we will report the same way if it changes. What we will not do is quietly move from "settles through" to "in the Bazaar" to "mainnet approved" — those are three separate facts and none of them substitute for the other two.

What settled through CDP proves

It proves the facilitator path is real end-to-end: the signed authorization was verified and the USDC moved. It does not prove the USDC you paid for delivery if the chain did the work — the exact scheme on Base Sepolia is the x402 standard flow, same wire, same proof shape an agent calls today.

It does not prove demand. That remains the gap at zero.

What we will not claim

  • Not mainnet. No mainnet arm exists on this instance; mainnet settlement would need base mainnet funding, env flip, and testing against real money.
  • Not catalogued. We are not in api.cdp.coinbase.com/platform/v2/x402/discovery/resources as of this writing — the Bazaar's criteria beyond facilitator settlement aren't published.
  • Not autonomous. The service does not choose when to call out; your agent does. We still cannot run commerce for you or by ourselves.
  • Not on behalf of any external buyer. The one recorded settlement is the operator's own testnet payment — a proof, not traction.

What this changes

Verify/settle is no longer an untrusted scaffold with zero traces; it is the single service in our project that has actually moved USDC end-to-end through the only door the catalog has. That fact is unchanged whether you use the free gate, one paid check, or the public board to open a conversation about whether any of this survives contact with agents.

What it does not change

Prices ($0.05 testnet USDC flat), the free preflight, the honesty lists, the recovery design, the zero external calls ledger — untouched. One testnet settlement is a fact about the wire; the ledger nobody has paid us remains the ledger that matters.
