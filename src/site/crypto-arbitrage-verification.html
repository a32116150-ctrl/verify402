Paid · $0.05 · mainnet measurement

Verify the spread before you fund the trade.

A claimed cross-venue arbitrage can be a stale quote, a gross spread that costs eat, or a real edge at one block only. verify402's /verify check measures the claim at a pinned block and prints one of three honest verdicts. It does not place a trade.

See the request shape Read the receipts

01 · How the measurement works

Router math, not a back-of-envelope spread.

At the pinned block, the check reads Uniswap's canonical QuoterV2 on one side and each venue's own router math on the other. It subtracts the flash-loan premium and gas before it answers. That order matters: a positive gross difference is not an edge if the costs are larger.

All quotes come from the protocols' own contracts. verify402 does not reimplement venue mathematics from a whitepaper and does not invent a number when a call fails.

02 · Three states, not one cheerleading flag

edge, no_edge, no_edge_costs_ate_it.

VerdictPlain meaning
edgeThe measured net survives the modeled costs at that block.
no_edgeThe opportunity was not there at that block.
no_edge_costs_ate_itA gross spread appeared, but premium and gas consumed it.

The live feed often returns no_edge. That is not a bug. A falsification service earns trust by saying no clearly.

03 · The other four measurements

A spread is only one part of the decision.

/route ($0.03) shows what the chain would actually pay across quoted venues. /quotecheck ($0.05) audits a quote someone gave you. /depth ($0.05) builds a capacity ladder so you know when impact eats the plan. /safety ($0.02) is the lead token-safety check with quote-based sellability.

/assure ($0.10) runs the checks you supply at one pinned block and returns one evidence record. It is accepted only when those checks cost at least $0.10 separately (parts total $0.12); unsupplied checks stay in notChecked.

04 · What this is not

A measurement is not an execution.

The result is a quote about one block. Prices move, liquidity changes, and a transaction that lands later can see a different state. verify402 never holds your keys, signs your trade, guarantees profit, or manages money. It is a measurement service, not financial advice.

Recompute the evidence

A paid response carries a request hash, a response hash and the pinned block. The receipt guide explains how those fields let a third party check the record instead of trusting the prose.

Before the paid call
